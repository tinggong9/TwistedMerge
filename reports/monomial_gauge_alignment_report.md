## Scope

- Monomial matching is implemented for one-hidden-layer ReLU `mlp` models and two-hidden-layer ReLU `mlp2` models.
- The gauge is permutation plus positive hidden-unit scaling with consistent adjacent-layer inverse scaling, so it is an exact ReLU reparameterization before averaging.
- The monomial merge row is a single same-capacity merged model, not an ensemble and not a period-index lift.
- The main fixed-setting obstruction claim gate is unchanged. This report only compares permutation and monomial diagnostics in rows generated with monomial matching modes.
- The monomial CSV artifacts are compact summaries: repeated raw permutation arrays and layerwise alignment JSON are omitted from these monomial-specific exports, while scalar residuals, functional-preservation metrics, scale-stability metrics, and paired deltas are retained.

## Conclusion

Supported gates: a same-capacity monomial row passes the positive paired-CI gate versus C2M3 in at least one fixed setting; no monomial row passes the greedy-soup win gate.

Raw activation-derived scales show large log scales or many low-similarity assignments; treat raw monomial activation rows as unreliable and prefer clipped/shrinkage/global rows.
