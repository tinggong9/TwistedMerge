We study model merging when local parameterizations are related by chart transformations whose pairwise maps need not be globally coherent. We introduce synchronization diagnostics, a weighted Hodge residual decomposition, conservative low-rank corrections, chart-aware routing, and invariant pooling. In controlled noncommutative context tasks, the structured method reaches mean accuracy 0.6378 compared with 0.5636 for the strongest generic context baseline (generic_mixture_of_experts) over the fixed discovery grid, and the narrow preregistered confirmation is executed. Compact natural-checkpoint and real-image frame experiments do not yet establish a realistic correction advantage. We therefore restrict the empirical claim to Level 2 and retain all negative findings.
