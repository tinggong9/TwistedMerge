# Shared-base transformer benchmark

Two collections of four partially fine-tuned checkpoints were trained from pinned `google/bert_uncased_L-2_H-128_A-2` revision `30b0a37ccaaa32f332884b96992754e246e48c5f` on four real sentiment domains. All predictions were executed. No residual certificate activated, so the conservative selector used an ordinary candidate and the full gate was **not passed**.
