\section{CIFAR and Bridge Boundary Appendix}
\label{app:cifar-bridge-boundary}

This appendix records CIFAR and bridge-dataset evidence as a boundary result.
It supports the limited claim that CIFAR is included as a bounded appendix
boundary, not as a broad model-merging win.  It does not support the claim that
CIFAR confirms the main method.

\paragraph{CIFAR gate chronology.}
The first CIFAR-10 gate failed: the initial probe reached test accuracy
\(0.2480\), below the \(0.45\) plumbing threshold and the \(0.60\)
meaningful-claim threshold.  A bounded rescue with a larger no-BatchNorm
two-convolution ReLU CNN, normalized inputs, longer training, and random
crop/flip augmentation cleared the base gate with max individual accuracy
\(0.658333\) on the merge setting.  This allowed diagnostics, but not a broad
method-win claim because the rescue had only one merge setting.

\paragraph{Final CIFAR result.}
The final confirmatory CIFAR run used the rescued no-BatchNorm architecture over
five seeds.  The base gate passed: mean individual max accuracy was \(0.650620\)
and the minimum setting max was \(0.646400\).  Exact channel gauges remained
descriptive.  Optimized channel scaling was only \(+0.000540\) versus
C2M3-style channel synchronization, with confidence interval
\([-0.000880,0.002740]\); shrinkage and global channel scaling were similarly
small and had intervals crossing zero.  Greedy soup remained the boundary:
greedy soup reached mean test accuracy \(0.648000\), C2M3-style channel
synchronization reached \(0.464420\), and the greedy-safe selector tied greedy
soup.  The union candidate soup was \(+0.000440\) over greedy soup with a
confidence interval touching zero.  The ensemble upper bound was higher but is
extra-capacity.

\paragraph{Bridge datasets.}
Rotated-MNIST and colored-MNIST are bridge datasets only.  They support checking
whether the C2M3-versus-greedy boundary pattern persists under simple
MNIST-derived shifts.  In the expanded bridge run, greedy soup beat C2M3-style
channel synchronization by \(+0.084724\) overall with confidence interval
\([0.063629,0.103986]\).  In the main rotated-25 \(N=3\) 10-seed setting, the
delta was \(+0.089670\), with confidence interval
\([0.067857,0.112412]\).  These results cannot be promoted to CIFAR or general
vision claims.

\paragraph{Claim boundary.}
Exact channel permutations and positive scales are exact ReLU
reparameterizations for the no-BatchNorm CNNs used here, but exactness of the
gauge does not imply an accuracy win after averaging independently trained
checkpoints.  CIFAR is therefore included as a concise appendix boundary.  It is
not evidence that CIFAR confirms the main method, and bridge datasets do not
imply CIFAR or general vision performance.
