\section{Results and Contributions}
\label{sec:results-contributions-after-35791f7}

Our experiments separate cohomological obstruction, exact same-architecture gauge correction, validation-based model selection, and extra-capacity lifts. This separation is important for the main claim boundary: TwistedMerge provides controlled mathematical obstruction witnesses and limited exact-gauge practical improvements, while greedy soup remains a strong boundary baseline and no broad CIFAR or official-external-baseline win is claimed.

\paragraph{Finite-site obstruction witness.}
The synthetic tetrahedron-boundary experiment gives a concrete nonzero \(H^2(\mu_2)\) obstruction. The rank-one sign-twist instance has locally and pairwise consistent data but no ordinary global descent: the nontrivial face product is not a coboundary and ordinary global synchronization fails. In the controlled experiment, a doubled representation absorbs the twist and recovers zero twisted loss. This is evidence for the obstruction mechanism itself, not evidence that real neural residuals are Brauer classes.

\paragraph{Planted obstruction and pairwise merge degradation.}
The planted MNIST MLP copy experiment tests whether cycle inconsistency can causally affect pairwise merging. In exact functional copies with one corrupted edge, Git-ReBasin-style pairwise alignment degradation increases with the planted defect level. For the central \(\mu_2\) family the degradation rises from \(0.0000\) to \(0.0387\), with Spearman correlation \(0.8741\). C2M3-style synchronization removes the planted inconsistency in the tested setting, while the rank-lift branch gives no gain beyond C2M3 and remains an extra-capacity construction.

\paragraph{MNIST MLP exact-gauge selector.}
On the faithful in-repository MNIST MLP external-baseline comparison, the improved validated selector improves over internal C2M3-style synchronization by paired mean test accuracy \(0.0209\), with bootstrap interval \([0.0135,0.0296]\). The same selector remains below faithful greedy soup by \(-0.0024\), with interval \([-0.0042,-0.0009]\). Thus the supported practical MNIST claim is a limited win over the in-repository C2M3-style baseline, not a greedy-soup win and not an official external-baseline result.

\paragraph{Fashion-MNIST MLP and CNN generalization.}
Fashion-MNIST shows that the exact-gauge improvement is not restricted to MNIST MLPs. In the one-hidden-layer ReLU MLP benchmark, optimized monomial scaling improves over C2M3-style permutation synchronization by paired mean \(0.0525\), and the improved validated selector improves by \(0.0664\). The selector is still below greedy soup by \(-0.0008\). In the no-BatchNorm Fashion-MNIST CNN benchmark, exact channel-gauge scaling variants also improve over C2M3-style channel synchronization: optimized channel scaling has paired mean delta \(0.009333\) versus C2M3. Greedy soup remains stronger in both Fashion-MNIST settings. The residual diagnostics continue to report zero finite-index and zero real central/projective candidate fractions under the tested structure groups.

\paragraph{Rotated and colored MNIST bridge datasets.}
The bridge experiments on rotated-MNIST and colored-MNIST test whether the boundary pattern persists away from the original MNIST MLP setting. In the main rotated-MNIST \(25^\circ\), \(N=3\) setting, optimized channel scaling improves over C2M3-style synchronization by \(0.018480\), while greedy soup is higher than C2M3 by \(0.089670\). Across bridge settings, optimized channel scaling beats C2M3 in the tested rows and greedy soup remains the dominant boundary baseline. These are MNIST-derived bridge results; they do not establish CIFAR or broad vision generality.

\paragraph{Final bounded CIFAR result.}
The final CIFAR-10 run is a bounded no-BatchNorm two-conv ReLU CNN experiment. It clears the base-accuracy gate: the mean individual maximum test accuracy is \(0.650620\), and every final setting passes the gate. The exact channel-gauge effects are descriptive rather than claim-promoting. Optimized channel scaling has mean delta \(0.000540\) versus C2M3-style channel synchronization with an interval crossing zero, while greedy soup is \(0.183580\) above C2M3. Union candidate soup is \(0.000440\) above greedy soup with an interval touching zero. Therefore CIFAR is included as an appendix boundary: the base-accuracy gate passes, but no robust CIFAR exact-gauge win or broad CIFAR model-merging claim is made.

\paragraph{Residual taxonomy and non-Brauer evidence.}
The residual taxonomy is useful because it blocks false promotion of ordinary noncentral residuals into scalar projective structure. Fashion-MNIST MLP, Fashion-MNIST CNN, bridge, and CIFAR diagnostics report zero finite-index candidate fractions and zero real central/projective candidate fractions in the relevant tested rows. The supported statement is that real residuals remain mostly noncentral or non-Brauer under the tested diagnostics. The unsupported statement is that these residuals are Brauer, projective, or period-index classes.

\paragraph{Period-index and time-frequency detectors.}
The controlled period-index experiments verify the expected rank thresholds. Period-only ranks are obstructed, while index-divisible ranks lift in the finite-Heisenberg benchmarks. Robust detector calibration and nearest-Heisenberg projection maintain zero accepted false-lift rate on negative controls when commutator-form checks, projection residual thresholds, robust centrality checks, and index-divisibility gates all pass. Time-frequency learned-chart and denoised-chart experiments show recovery of controlled finite-Heisenberg structure under these gates. These results support the detector stack and residual-gated lift policy; they do not imply that arbitrary neural residuals contain period-index structure.

\paragraph{Block-gauge phase diagram and residual gate.}
The block-gauge phase diagram shows why connection residual must gate any projected cycle score. Exact global block gauges and learned noncontiguous block positive controls are accepted under strict calibration, while fake projection traps, noncentral block holonomy, and scalar block-phase rows are rejected or separately flagged. In an exact linear-hidden block-compatible architecture, optimized block-gauge aligned averaging improves over unaligned weight averaging, but real ReLU block rotations remain diagnostic-only. The block-gauge result therefore strengthens the residual taxonomy rather than adding a broad ReLU or CIFAR merge claim.

\paragraph{Official external baselines and NSD status.}
Official Git-ReBasin, C2M3, and Model Soups repositories were cloned or inspected, with licenses and commits recorded, but direct runs on the exact MNIST MLP checkpoint set were infeasible because of environment, framework, and architecture mismatches. Consequently the paper reports faithful in-repository baseline comparisons and makes no official-external-baseline win claim. The official Neural Sheaf Diffusion integration is a tiny smoke run with post-hoc TwistedMerge cycle diagnostics; it is optional related evidence, not a broad GNN or sheaf-regularization benchmark.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
The current practical contribution is a limited exact-gauge improvement over faithful internal C2M3-style synchronization on MNIST and Fashion-MNIST settings. Greedy soup remains a strong boundary baseline and is not beaten by the central validated selectors in the supported paired comparisons. CIFAR passes a bounded no-BatchNorm base-accuracy gate, but exact channel-gauge effects are descriptive only. Branch, projective, finite-index, and ensemble lifts add capacity unless separately compressed and should not be presented as capacity-matched single-model merges. Finally, real neural residuals have not been certified as Brauer or period-index classes; the present evidence supports conservative noncentral/non-Brauer taxonomy rather than a positive Brauer claim.
