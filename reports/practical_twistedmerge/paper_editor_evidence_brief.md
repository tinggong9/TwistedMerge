# Practical TwistedMerge paper-editor evidence brief

Status: audit complete; controlled LoRA smoke not yet executed.

## New evidence available now

No new empirical result is available at the audit stage. The audit isolates a missing test: repeated merging of one fixed set of effective LoRA updates represented in many equivalent rank-space gauges.

## Existing boundaries retained

- The selector-attribution phase is negative; it does not support a TwistedMerge-specific ordinary-merge gain.
- Greedy soup remains a strong accuracy boundary.
- Existing BatchNorm results are narrow functional identities, not trained-ResNet merging performance.
- The previous four-task BERT LoRA gate failed and cannot support a positive adapter-merging claim.
- Effective-delta averaging plus fixed-rank SVD is an essential gauge-invariant baseline.
- No natural Brauer, period-index, clinical, or broad SOTA statement is supported.

## Editorial action

Do not change the manuscript from this audit alone. If the controlled smoke passes, its first acceptable wording will be limited to synthetic numerical invariance under well-conditioned planted LoRA rank-space transformations. Any real-model or performance wording remains gated on licensed, independently trained adapter groups and a frozen confirmatory protocol.
