# Holonomy Applications Negative Results

## Invalid evidence kept out

The historical controlled nonabelian performance tables remain quarantined. Their label-derived target-accuracy construction was not reused in any score, selector, plot, or application claim. New A and C logits were produced by executed models, saved and hashed before test labels were accessed, and checked by label-permutation guards.

## Application A: no practical invariant-pooling win

- The held-out overlap criterion selected the weight-derived transition in all five seeds. Its loop products are approximately identity because `Q_ij = A_j pinv(A_i)` is near a coboundary by construction.
- Activation-Procrustes loops are nonidentity, with mean per-seed maximum loop distance `0.6833`, but mean held-out residual `0.8447` and bootstrap instability `0.4905` prevent a stable natural-holonomy claim.
- Orbit invariant pooling loses to generic MoE by `-0.0160` accuracy, 95% group-bootstrap interval `[-0.0200, -0.0136]`.
- It loses to the branch-count-matched random control by `-0.0062`, interval `[-0.0112, -0.0020]`, and to D4 test-time augmentation by `-0.0104`, interval `[-0.0150, -0.0064]`.
- Its worst-view accuracy improves over generic MoE but remains below D4 TTA, so the required worst-view-without-mean-loss gate fails.
- No new dataset or chart family was opened.

## Application B: no natural central projective class

- All five Application-A-selected connections are `trivial_coboundary`.
- All fifteen alternative seed-estimator settings are `noncentral_holonomy`.
- These labels are identical under strict, medium, and loose tolerance policies.
- No natural row passes centrality, torsion, cocycle consistency, nontriviality modulo coboundaries, gauge invariance, bootstrap stability, and predicted lift behavior together.
- Therefore no natural candidate is called a Brauer-like candidate or Brauer class.

## Application C: structure does not imply a superior planner

- The planted period/index cases recover exact relations and actual logits at divisible capacities.
- The coherent lift ties the parameter-matched generic unitary control exactly in every predicted-index comparison; each paired delta is `0.0`.
- Several index-insufficient truncations match or exceed ensemble classification accuracy while failing exact relations or unitarity. Task accuracy alone therefore does not identify the index.
- The controlled structural threshold is supported, but the practical-superiority gate is negative.

## Application D: mixed-regime separation is not a natural linter gain

- The strict double-holdout protocol excludes both the test seed and its entire setting family from training.
- Across all 680 mixed natural/controlled rows, full features improve AUROC by `0.0993`, interval `[0.0364, 0.1740]`, and reduce Brier score by `0.0458`.
- On the 80 natural Application A rows, however, the full diagnostic changes AUROC by `-0.0273`, interval `[-0.0429, -0.0057]`, and AUPRC by `-0.0073`, whose interval crosses zero.
- The mixed improvement can separate evidence regimes and controlled capacity states; it cannot support a natural mergeability claim.
- Gauge-synchronization sufficiency has zero positive confirmatory examples and is explicitly `inadequate_sample`.
