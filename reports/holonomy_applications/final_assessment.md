# Final Assessment: Holonomy Applications

## Overall decision

The focused program is complete. It supports a conservative audit/rejection workflow and a controlled structural period-index check, but it does not establish a positive practical holonomy application on the natural adapter corpus. Further application expansion should stop under the preregistered rules.

## 1. Did loop holonomy add information beyond pairwise alignment?

Not in a supported natural application sense. Activation-derived transitions show nonidentity and noncommuting loop operators that are not visible in a single pairwise residual, but they have worse held-out fit and high bootstrap instability. The strict natural-family linter test finds no incremental discrimination gain over pairwise-plus-synchronization features; natural-only AUROC changes by `-0.0273`, 95% cell-bootstrap interval `[-0.0429, -0.0057]`.

## 2. Was any natural central projective/Brauer-like class certified?

No. Zero natural seed-estimator settings are central finite-order candidates. The term `Brauer class` is not warranted.

## 3. Were natural residuals predominantly central or noncentral?

The five held-out-selected weight-derived connections are trivial/coboundary. Among the fifteen nontrivial alternative seed-estimator settings, all fifteen are noncentral at strict, medium, and loose thresholds. Thus the nontrivial natural residuals are predominantly noncentral, not central projective.

## 4. Did invariant pooling have a practical benefit?

No. Orbit invariant pooling loses to generic MoE by `0.0160` accuracy and to the matched random branch control by `0.0062`; both confidence intervals exclude zero in the unfavorable direction. It also trails D4 test-time augmentation. The preregistered practical gate fails.

## 5. Did period-index predict the minimum successful capacity on the real task?

Only in the planted controlled structural sense. For period/index `(2,2)`, `(2,4)`, and `(3,3)`, the index equals the minimum capacity at which exact projective relations, unitarity, and preserved actual logits all pass, for all five seeds. This is `controlled_on_real_features`, not natural discovery. Task accuracy alone does not reveal the threshold because some structurally invalid capacities still classify well.

## 6. Did the predicted threshold outperform parameter-matched generic capacity?

No. The coherent predicted-index lift and the parameter-matched generic unitary carrier have paired accuracy delta exactly `0.0` for every case and seed. The practical-superiority condition fails.

## 7. Did holonomy-aware auditing improve merge/abstain decisions?

No natural claim is supported. The full diagnostic improves mixed-regime AUROC from `0.7161` to `0.8152`, but that gain partly separates natural and controlled regimes. On natural rows alone it worsens AUROC from `0.5979` to `0.5636`. The conservative natural-family gate therefore fails.

## 8. What is the strongest application supported?

The strongest supported application is conservative certification: the pipeline reliably distinguishes a trivial/coboundary selected connection from noncentral alternatives and refuses to promote either to a natural Brauer-like class. The controlled period-index component is also a valid structural validator, but not a superior practical capacity planner.

## 9. Which results belong in the main paper?

No new positive application result belongs in the main paper. If the paper benefits from negative evidence, include at most a concise main-text boundary and place the corpus protocol, negative invariant-pooling result, conservative certificate taxonomy, controlled structural threshold, and linter evidence-boundary analysis in an appendix or artifact supplement.

## 10. Should further application experiments stop?

Yes. Application A failed its positive gate, so the preregistered rule forbids adding another chart family or dataset. B found no natural candidate, C found no advantage over generic capacity, and D found no natural incremental linter value. More flexible models, extra datasets, or broader sweeps would be post-hoc expansion rather than completion of this focused program.
