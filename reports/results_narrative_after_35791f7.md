# Results And Contributions After 35791f7

This narrative is intended as a paper-ready synthesis of the current TwistedMerge evidence after the final bounded CIFAR channel-gauge run, the rotated/colored-MNIST bridge expansion, and the unified quantitative obstruction chain. It deliberately states only artifact-supported claims.

## Main Contributions

TwistedMerge separates three questions that are easy to conflate: whether a residual is a genuine scalar/projective obstruction, whether an exact same-architecture gauge can improve a merge, and whether a validation-selected candidate beats strong averaging baselines. The current evidence supports a limited but coherent story.

First, controlled finite-site experiments give a concrete nonzero `H^2(mu_2)` obstruction witness. On the tetrahedron-boundary complex, all local and pairwise checks can be trivial while the global rank-one descent fails; a doubled representation absorbs the sign twist in the controlled setting. This is a mathematical and synthetic witness, not evidence that real MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, or CIFAR residuals are Brauer classes.

Second, planted-obstruction model-merging experiments show that cycle inconsistency can causally degrade pairwise alignment. In exact-copy MNIST MLPs with a planted inconsistent edge, Git-ReBasin-style pairwise alignment degrades monotonically with the planted defect level, while C2M3-style synchronization removes the planted inconsistency in the tested setting. The rank-lift branch does not improve beyond C2M3 there and remains an extra-capacity branch/ensemble construction.

Third, exact ReLU-compatible gauges give limited practical improvements over faithful in-repository C2M3-style baselines. On MNIST MLPs, the improved validated selector improves over internal C2M3-style synchronization by about `0.0209` test accuracy in the external-baseline comparison, while remaining below faithful greedy soup by about `0.0024`. On Fashion-MNIST MLPs, optimized monomial scaling and the improved validated selector improve over C2M3-style synchronization; the improved selector has paired mean test delta `0.0664` versus C2M3, but `-0.0008` versus greedy soup. Thus the practical claim is a limited exact-gauge/C2M3 improvement, not a greedy-soup or external-baseline win.

Fourth, the exact-gauge story extends beyond MNIST MLP in bounded ways. On Fashion-MNIST CNNs without BatchNorm, exact channel permutation and positive channel scaling are same-architecture ReLU CNN symmetries. Shrinkage, global, and optimized channel scaling improve over C2M3-style channel permutation synchronization, with optimized scaling giving paired mean delta `0.009333` versus C2M3. Greedy soup remains much stronger, and greedy-safe/union candidate soups mainly match or slightly edge greedy soup with confidence intervals touching zero. On Fashion-MNIST MLPs and CNNs, residual diagnostics remain useful and continue to reject central/projective interpretations under the tested structure groups.

Fifth, rotated-MNIST and colored-MNIST bridge datasets confirm the C2M3-versus-greedy boundary pattern between MNIST-like and CIFAR-like difficulty. In the main rotated-MNIST 25-degree setting, optimized channel scaling improves over C2M3-style synchronization by `0.018480`, while greedy soup is higher by `0.089670` over C2M3. Across bridge settings, optimized channel scaling beats C2M3 in the tested rows, but greedy soup remains the dominant boundary baseline. These bridge results are MNIST-derived and are not promoted to broad CIFAR or general-vision claims.

Sixth, the final CIFAR-10 no-BatchNorm experiment clears the base-accuracy gate but closes the CIFAR branch as an appendix boundary. The bounded two-conv ReLU CNN reaches mean individual maximum accuracy `0.650620`, with every final setting passing the base-accuracy gate. Exact channel-gauge effects are tiny and descriptive: optimized channel scaling has mean delta `0.000540` versus C2M3-style channel synchronization with a confidence interval crossing zero, while greedy soup is about `0.183580` above C2M3. Union candidate soup has a small mean delta `0.000440` versus greedy soup with a confidence interval touching zero. The supported CIFAR claim is therefore that the plumbing and base-accuracy gate pass in a bounded no-BatchNorm setting; no broad CIFAR exact-gauge win is claimed.

Seventh, the residual taxonomy remains useful because it prevents overclaiming. Across Fashion-MNIST MLP/CNN and CIFAR channel diagnostics, finite-index candidate fractions and real central/projective candidate fractions are zero in the relevant reports, while noncentral or non-Brauer residual labels dominate under the tested structure groups. The taxonomy supports statements such as "real residuals remain noncentral/non-Brauer under tested diagnostics"; it does not support "real residuals are Brauer or period-index classes."

Eighth, the period-index and finite-Heisenberg results are strong controlled mathematics and diagnostics. The central period-index benchmarks verify the rank threshold: period-only ranks are obstructed, while index-divisible ranks lift. Robust detector calibration and nearest finite-Heisenberg projection preserve false-lift rate zero on negative controls when commutator-form checks, residual gates, and rank-divisibility checks are enforced. Time-frequency chart recovery and projection experiments show that learned or denoised charts can recover controlled finite-Heisenberg structure when the residual gate accepts. These results justify the detector stack, not a claim that arbitrary neural residuals contain period-index structure.

Ninth, the block-gauge phase diagram identifies connection residual as the necessary gate. Exact global block gauges and learned noncontiguous positive controls are accepted under strict calibration, while fake projection traps, noncentral block holonomy, and scalar block phases are rejected or separately flagged. Optimized block-compatible aligned averaging improves over unaligned weight averaging in an exact linear-hidden architecture, but remains diagnostic-only for real ReLU block rotations and does not establish a natural ReLU or CIFAR block-gauge win.

Finally, the external-code status is intentionally conservative. Official Git-ReBasin, C2M3, and Model Soups repositories were cloned or inspected, licenses and commits were recorded, and direct runs on the exact MNIST MLP checkpoint set were blocked by framework, architecture, or environment mismatch. Therefore all practical baseline claims are against faithful in-repository implementations only. The official Neural Sheaf Diffusion integration is a tiny smoke/diagnostic run with post-hoc cycle analysis, not a general sheaf-regularization or model-merging benchmark.

## Manuscript Claim Boundary

The paper can safely be framed as a mathematical and diagnostic model-merging paper with limited exact-gauge practical wins. The central positive story is not "TwistedMerge beats greedy soup" or "TwistedMerge solves CIFAR." It is:

1. controlled cohomological and period-index obstruction experiments establish precise failure and lift conditions;
2. exact same-architecture gauges improve over faithful internal C2M3-style synchronization in limited MNIST/Fashion-MNIST MLP/CNN settings;
3. greedy soup remains a strong boundary baseline and is usually stronger than the exact-gauge selectors;
4. bridge datasets reproduce the boundary pattern without justifying broad vision claims;
5. CIFAR passes the bounded no-BatchNorm base-accuracy gate but supplies only descriptive exact-gauge effects;
6. real residuals remain noncentral/non-Brauer under the tested diagnostics.

## Limitations

The current practical wins are limited to faithful in-repository baselines and exact same-architecture gauge families. No official external baseline result is available. No broad CIFAR, ImageNet, or SOTA model-merging claim is supported. Greedy soup remains a stronger or matching baseline in the main practical comparisons unless a paired confidence interval explicitly supports otherwise, which the current central runs do not. Branch lifts, projective lifts, and ensembles add capacity and should not be described as capacity-matched single merged models. Real neural residuals have not been certified as Brauer, projective, or period-index classes; the detector stack is valuable precisely because it refuses those claims when the evidence is absent.
