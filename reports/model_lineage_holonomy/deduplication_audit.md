## Why this experiment is distinct

Prior D4 work compared models indexed by a known finite group action. The present graph is generated endogenously by learning history:

`M0 -> task adaptation -> ordered task adaptation -> terminal learning path`.

Its loops close different task orders and branch histories, including adjacent swaps such as `ABC` versus `BAC`. The practical outcomes are task-order sensitivity, forgetting, harmful same-base branch merging, and conservative abstention. No chart group, planted projective carrier, artificial gauge scramble, or method-specific target is introduced.

This distinction is substantive: pairwise endpoints can be close while the learned transports along two histories disagree. The preregistered tests ask whether that higher-order disagreement is stable and useful after parameter distance, prediction disagreement, and pairwise transport residual are controlled.

## Compute estimate

The complete graph has 16 checkpoints per independent seed: one `M0`, three single-task nodes, six ordered two-task nodes, and six three-task permutations. The five-seed confirmatory target is 80 checkpoint records, of which five `M0` states are reused and 75 lightweight children are trained.

Historical evidence on this same machine extracted roughly 40,000 frozen ResNet views in 17.0 seconds and trained 40 rank-4 adapters in about 4.1 aggregate seconds. The new extraction has about 14,300 corrupted image views plus three pooled hook layers, and the sequential graph has more optimizer segments and transport fits. The conservative estimate is:
